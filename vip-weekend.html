What is the VIP Weekend?

The VIP Weekend is a once-a-year opportunity for fans to spend meaningful time with Dwight in a relaxed, celebratory environment. Unlike a traditional concert, this experience is intentionally limited in size to allow for real interaction, shared moments, and a deeper connection to the music and community.

Guests attend multiple events across three days, including live shows, exclusive gatherings, and informal social experiences not available to the general public.

What’s Included

  • Access to all scheduled VIP Weekend events
  • Live performances featuring Dwight Icenhower
  • Welcome party and closing celebration
  • Meet & greet opportunities with photos
  • Q&A sessions and informal fan gatherings
  • Exclusive VIP Weekend keepsake / laminate

Not Included

  • Hotel accommodations
  • Transportation (airfare, car rental, etc.)
  • Meals not specifically listed as part of an event
